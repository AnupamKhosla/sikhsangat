    No, you get ads, but you can only get the option to be monetized after you reach a certain goal. 

    when you upload a video they immediately give you a choice to monetise or not. Monetisation isn't linked to sub count. If the video contains easily identifiable copyrighted material that YouTube's algorithm can detect as not the intellectual property of the uploader, the video is blocked or restricted unless you're creating a video essay / analysis in which case the Fair Use aspect comes into play. I don't think YouTube's detection bots are capable of discerning shabads and their original owners / performers; it struggles with English in a non-standard dialect. That's why there's a load of Sikhs in India who are making God knows how much money by just uploading MP3s of kirtan and katha.
