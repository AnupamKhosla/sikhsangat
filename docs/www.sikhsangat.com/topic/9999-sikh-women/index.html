    .: Vaheguru Ji Ka Khalsa Vaheguru Ji Ki Fateh :.

    Sikh Women

    6pm Wednesday 9th March 2005

    Bennett Lecture Theatre 2

    University of Leicester,

    University Road,

    Leicester,

    LE1 7RH

    View Campus Map

    View Map

    University of Leicester Sikh Society will be holding a presentation about SIKH WOMEN.

    This is in conjunction with various remembrance days for prominent Sikh Women but also to celebrate 'International Women's Day'. The presentation will focus on prominent Sikh women and focus on the role of women in Sikhism.

    This presentation is welcome to all.

    It is taking place on Wednesday 9th March 2005 from 6pm in Bennett Lecture Theatre 2.
