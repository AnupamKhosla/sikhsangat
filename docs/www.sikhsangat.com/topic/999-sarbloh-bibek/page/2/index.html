    I have heard there are 4 or 5 levels of Bibeks. The first level of Bibek is just keeping all of your kakkars. I forgot the second or third level (bad memory). But the second last level of bibek is when you only eat from other bibeki Gursikhs, this is what most bibekis follow. The last level of bibek is “Sarbloh Bibek” whereby you only eat food that is prepared from Sarbloh and you only eat out of sarbloh. Sarbloh Bibek is perhaps the least common out of all of them. I don’t keep it, maybe a few years down the road, as I don’t know how to cook.

    M Khalsa and Khalistani,

    Sant Jarnail Singh Jee kept bibek, the second last level of bibek I have mentioned above. He did not keep “Sarbloh bibek”. But Sant Gurbachan Singh Jee kept Sarboh bibek, which is the final level of bibek.
